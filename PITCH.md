# DebateGPT - AI-Powered Debate Training Platform

DebateGPT solves the critical problem that debate teams, students, and professionals face: **finding a skilled debate partner who's available 24/7 to practice with**. Traditional debate practice requires coordinating schedules, finding opponents at your skill level, and getting constructive feedback—all significant barriers to improvement. DebateGPT eliminates these obstacles by providing an intelligent AI opponent that adapts to your skill level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced), engages in real-time argumentation on any topic, and delivers structured feedback analyzing your logical strength, evidence quality, persuasiveness, and argument structure.

What makes DebateGPT unique is its **dual-AI architecture**: one Claude model serves as your adaptive debate opponent while another analyzes each of your arguments in real-time, providing actionable feedback with specific strengths and improvement areas. This creates a complete training loop where you not only practice making arguments but immediately learn what worked and what didn't. The platform tracks your progress across debate rounds, supports keyboard shortcuts for rapid engagement, and lets you export your debate sessions for review or sharing.

This is technically impressive because it demonstrates advanced AI orchestration—managing two separate Claude conversations (debate and analysis) while maintaining state, streaming responses in real-time, and generating structured feedback using Zod schemas. The app leverages the latest Vercel AI SDK with custom transport layers, Next.js 15 with Turbopack for blazing-fast development, and shadcn/ui for a polished, accessible interface. It's production-ready with full TypeScript type safety, responsive design, and a clean architecture that separates concerns between debate logic, feedback systems, and UI components.

Whether you're a high school debater preparing for competition, a law student honing persuasion skills, or a professional improving presentation abilities, DebateGPT provides an always-available, judgment-free space to develop critical thinking and argumentation skills that transfer directly to real-world success.
